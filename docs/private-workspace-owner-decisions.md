# Owner decision register: identity and private-workspace proposals

**Status: pending decisions, not ratification.** Baseline `bb166fa` includes merged PR #219, whose text explicitly does not ratify ADR §14 or §15. This register records unresolved choices; it does not invent owner answers. No persistence, session or workspace policy is activated here.

## What is already recorded

- [ADR §12](./private-workspace-security-architecture.md#12-decisions-requiring-owner-ratification) is the authoritative historical D1–D11 record. D1–D10 are adopted; **D11 remains provisional**, requiring counsel and DPIA confirmation. Do not renumber or reinterpret those decisions in this register.
- Per the owner's statements, ORAtlas is an owner-operated PoC; personal experiments and independent-researcher private-workspace testing are intended. The owner reports no private data yet; this is not an independent audit.
- The synthetic-only initial content restriction, hosted notice and readiness conditions are **proposed in §15**, not confirmed policy. Platform ownership does not confer rights in other people's data.

## How to respond

For example: `I1: accept recommendation; I2: revise — ...; I3: defer — ...; A14.1–A14.6: accept as written; A15: revise — ...`. Separately state unresolved I3 lifecycle details, I4 retention decisions and whether S1 design work should proceed. Do not provide credentials or private research. Accepted answers must be recorded with their actual source/date in a follow-up change; do not treat this template as an approval.
